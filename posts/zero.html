September 2026

Earlier this summer, Juli and I were participating in another Two Minutes Hate about one of her friends, who was so obsessed with K-pop that they (not Korean) minored in Korean and studied abroad in Korea. Obviously we're allowed to hate on this a little bit. It's objectively weird. But I'm also pretty sure I'm doing the exact same thing. So obsessed with One Direction that I uprooted my entire life to live my tumblr Anglophilic dreams. Keeping calm and carrying on, if you will.
So much for that. There are few Anglos to be found in the northeast of Scotland, a famously Celtic nation. (Apparently that's a good thing.) Lowkey, though, everyone is so friendly here. I know we like to blame the whole Seattle freeze thing on the shitty weather but I guess we're just regular old assholes.

I assumed my inital indigestion was caused by my dad's tendency to order the Hindu vegetarian meal on every flight (PLEASE let me eat white people food! They made it for this exact situation!). But my stomach actually dropped when we got to the Aberdeen airport. There is only one baggage claim. Singular. The airport is international only because they have one daily flight to Dublin. To top it off, our luggage isn't on this singular baggage claim since they only handle domestic baggage here. This, combined with my cursory Wikipedia research—supposedly this city is 90% white—did not bode well for the next 3 years.

It's Saturday, and school doesn't start until Thursday. Cue the jetlag-fueled insanity spiral. Trapped in this hotel room and the only people I can talk to are my dad and the people in my phone from the hours of 5-8:30 AM. I'm sleeping so crazy that I've forgotten about the time difference. Are all my friends ignoring me?

My fault for spending the last four months in the glue trap called "parents' house." My grandmother had been feeding my sister and me hand to mouth like we were fat little ducks being gavaged so they could harvest our livers for foie gras. (I wouldn't eat my liver unless you really fucked with $3 Victory Lap margaritas.) There is a comfort in being a fat little duck, though. You know your purpose in life, however unpleasant that may be. I'd spent enough weeks at my Uniqlo summer job: I know the Six Standard Phrases like the back of my hand, and trust that I can A-rank some Jersey Easy Barrel Pants. I can spend the rest of my life working towards the Uniqlo standard of folding 7 T-shirts a minute.
Every second of that job, however, I felt like I was destined for something greater. Some white-collar job befitting someone of my pedigree and stature. I know they'll eat me during the class war. Hope you enjoy foie gras, queers!

Unfortunately, no, one cannot watch 30 Rock for the rest of their life. There are only 7 seasons. And I have two fucking degrees that I (kinda) worked hard for. Those have to be good for something. I've smoked so much weed and spent so long at home that I've practically forgotten that I ever went to college, but it had to be good for something.
There isn't another semester to relearn what a Hamiltonian is—undergrad is Actually, Totally over. I already miss Tweedy's and $30 concerts (ridiculous because there are a million Tweedy's over here) but those things are only in Austin only with your undergrad friends and you'll have to find new things to enjoy here. Or I can kill myself and die!

Some affirmations to tide you over:
  • Right now, you have a minor cold. You will not be sick for the rest of your life.
  • There is a time difference. Your friends are not ignoring you.
  • At least you're not in an American college town.
  • Northeastern Scotland is cooler than Manhattan.
  • You will make some awesome friends. You always do.
  • Now you can spend all your time outside (when the sun isn't setting at 3 pm. This sucked about Austin!
  • You can leave. There is so much to see in Europe.
  • This shit CLEARS Raleigh. You made the right choice.
  • You are finally working towards the only actual dream you've ever had for realsies.
  • The baddest bitches ain't in LA.

Whatever. There's no going back now! Anything can be the true system path if you believe it in your heart! (clearly, my A in classical dynamics was undeserved...)
