What does THAT mean?

Be proud of me more! Things are finally getting serious at work—I had to present at group meeting for the first time this week. Maybe some of you think that the Ph.D life is high-flying and busy. It is not. I have exactly obligations a week: a one-on-one with my supervisor Wednesday mornings, and then the whole lab group meets on Thursdays. And mind you, only two members present per week, so that's only once in every three group meetings. So most of my days are unguided meditations on the world at atomic scale, set to Brian Eno ambient music. Or One Direction if I'm feeling uncreative. (It's hard to curate the right vibe.) It's especially difficult considering that I only have like a solid 2 hours of productivity. I hate meditating.

Anyway, I had to present something this week, so I made a few slides about this new model I've been messing with. It essentially AI generates a bunch of crystal structures based on some minor prompting. Like Claude for materials (but significantly less patronizing). It's my job to figure out which creations are hallucinations and which ones can actually exist, but since my actual job is to find new materials, this makes my life easier.
But in order to understand this, you require some understanding of diffusion models. The best example of a diffusion model is probably those old DALL-E image generators that would make those really trippy images of underwater teddy bears and shit. So, this topic's a bit of a PowerPoint layup, but I managed to get a Boris Johnson eating fish collage and this image of White Obama in my 8-slide presentation.

Jokes aside, White Obama is the best illustration of how bias in the data set can affect the results of your diffusion model! This is what happens when you train your dataset on images of white people only.
And this is how I feel now that I've trained my sense of humor on a dataset of only chronically online gay people. I'm so out of my element right now. I'm literally a full-time researcher and I haven't been able to say "Mama, let's research" ONCE. Am I supposed to make everyone around me gay, or is it finally time to become trade?
