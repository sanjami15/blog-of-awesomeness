Two

7 October 2026

Look, I'll be the first to admit that I'm not good at making sound housing decisions. We all remember when I lived in that apartment where my bedroom had no windows. And we all remember when I re-signed my lease there for some goddamn reason. And of course I was depressed. You're probably like "nooo, it's not your fault! You're clinically depressed!" But I think that's unfair to clinics: there wasn't much they could've done. This was a problem only my realtor could solve.

And god bless University Realty Dave for helping us navigate the annals of pre-high rise West Campus. Our last apartment had private bathrooms and massive mirrors and windows (!!!) and a balcony so large it was practically a porch. Life is beautiful when it's a crisp 75°F on a Texas February night and you're sitting in a circle on your porch-balcony with your best friends, smoking a bowl of some "Rainbow Zkittlez" shit smuggled in from Colorado. Croix Condos #304 was a sparsely furnished IKEA boypartment, but it really did feel like home when I snuggled into bed, leaving the lights on because Nachiket made me watch some freaky horror film (I STILL vomit about when the kid in Bring Her Back bites straight into the wooden table).

But it's cold and rainy here. And weed is illegal. And I don't really have a TV. I'm pirating Babylon on my laptop. It's inspiring. I'm watching it as I orchestrate my big becoming-famous-pipe-dream move to LA (by way of a chemistry postdoc at UCLA because that's the craziest my dreams can get). Thanks to my old-ass landlord Garry for sending my flatmates my number and thanks to them for adding me to the Whatsapp groupchat a few days ago. But why did nobody text me until I sent a halfhearted Hello nice to live with y'all and why is the chat deader than roadkill now. Are Americans the only people that hang out?

Shoutout to Jesse for helping me take my suitcases upstairs and for being the first person to text me and the only person to hang out with me, though. He paired his "Hey, I'm Jesse" text with a Whatsapp handshake emoji with one black hand shaking one white hand [He is black]. WHAT DOES THAT MEAN. Whatsapp is lowkey that one friend that's too woke for including a factorial! of handshake emojis. I was cool with the Homer Simpson yellow one... now I'm playing racial mind games, wondering if I look white in my Whatsapp profile pic. (I don't.)

One more shoutout to Jesse for buying me a pint at the undergrad bar. The floors were sticky. I miss that all our shitty bars in Austin were outside. Drunk-spilling your margarita into a turf felt no more consequential than a dog pissing into a neighbor's yard. Now I feel bad for the custodial staff. On top of this it was like freshers' karaoke night and they were singing the worst songs America had to offer. For god's sake. Pick your own country's songs???

^This is only here because I am mildly proud of myself for figuring out how to embed Spotify tracks into HTML. And of course these Brits sang the shitty Darius Rucker cover instead of the actual one. Actually, I was so gagged when that song came up on the karaoke screen. How do they know about this song?!? There is absolutely no way they've been on a southbound train to Raleigh, North Carolina. They don't even know where that is. I immediately texted all my Southerner friends back in the States and we basked in pure American cultural dominance for a few perfect minutes.

As usual, I can never feel happy and present in the moment, so I spent my time here reminiscing about all the avant-garde karaoke in Austin (Little Big Town's Girl Crush in the sorority girl bar, singing Kyoto at the weed food truck for 20% off Avery's first bong, watching some gutsy trans girl solo all five minutes of "Cold Blows the Wind" by Ween at Calamity's on Guad). But I texted Luella the next day, and she told me all the normies had taken over Calamity's. There's no room on the songlist to sing Pet Shop Boys, and even if you made it on the list, they'll look at you weird. Perhaps it really was time to move on.
