Five

1 November 2026

Last night might have been my worst ever night out. And I've had a fair amount of bad nights (mostly ending in vomit). But vomit can be cleaned up. I was probably having fun before I vomited. Ugh. Halloween is supposed to be awesome.

It started out decent. I went to grad student coffee like every other Saturday, where I chatted to some 30 year old Canadian gay guy about the Aberdonian vibe, which is pretty much just grey at this time of year. He came to Europe to study ethics in biomedical science, because apparently they don't fund ethics research in North America. I'm not surprised. And then I went to the mall—I just got paid here for the first time! So I bought this random guy's riso print from the mall art gallery (is a mall art gallery cool or a little sad?) and I looked up some frames on Etsy to display it in my nonexistent apartment. I've been on Etsy a lot recently because the flat I chose is kind of ugly so I need ways to brighten it up. I'm taking suggestions. If you're wondering why I chose the ugly flat, it's because this one allows pets. Now, I don't have a pet. I'm not 100% about the soundness of choosing a flat based on a hypothetical dog, but unfortunately, I've pictured the dog, named it, and even anthropomorphized it. I'm getting that dog.
Next, I used my dad's card on a striped t-shirt that didn't really need to be £30. And I definitely don't need another striped t-shirt. My sister tells me they make me look like Charlie Spring from Heartstopper. I can hear my sister's voice every time I put one on: "Charlie! Charlie, why are we like this?" (Also, I need to figure out which gay bar they visit in the Heartstopper movie while I'm in the UK. That was some Berghain level shit. What the fuck do you mean they live in KENT?)
And finally I bought a Pandora charm for my mom. She collects them on my dime, so she picks out ones that are related to my life. Unfortunately, all the Scotland themed ones are kind of ugly, so I just bought her the dangly one of the Scottish flag. The Texas one was way cuter! It had a bluebonnet and a flag and a little Alamo. Anyway, I figured buying something for my mom would be good karma for the rest of my weekend.

And initially I thought I was right! I get home and run into the one roommate I talk to, and he asks me if I want to go grab a Halloween beer downtown. I don't have a costume, but neither does he. And some of his friends might even join us! This sounds amazing, considering I spent all of last night wistfully tapping through Instagram stories. Gay Halloween is still horribly derivative and far too reliant on niche references... but I don't think I've seen a gay guy since I left Austin. The situation is dire.

So the first warning sign—we're waiting at the bus stop, and I ask him who these friends we're meeting are. His response: "Don't judge me. She's not a prostitute." ??????????????????????????????? WHAT?????? HELLO?
Turns out they met on Hinge. Why the FUCK would prostitute even enter our lexicon right now.
And a second warning sign—about 15 minutes into our 40 minute walk (we missed the bus), he informs me that he drank four Buzzballs before we left. FOUR FUCKING BUZZBALLS. He just housed well over a bottle of wine all alone in his bedroom. Pregaming a casual beer with ten shots #okay! Whatever! Not my business!

Anyway. We make it to Triplekirks (here in Scotland there have always been a million Kirks), we have a pint, and he's texting some of his law school friends who are also out. He's lowkey too drunk to understand their voice notes, but we head out to a different bar. At this point, I'm lost—are we meeting the Hinge date or the law school friends? We end up at some kind of club. I didn't know this city had clubs, so this is a big deal. But the club is inundated with 18 year olds (it has the EXACT same rancid ass vibe as Aquarium on Dirty 6th in Austin. You know it's bad when the whole street is called 'dirty.') and it's almost impossible to order a drink, so I end up paying for an extra pint of beer. That's okay. I need to catch up to my flatmate anyway.
We move to a table outside and I'm enjoying my Corona. I miss Texas. And then some random girl picks up the extra Guinness and CHUGS IT. Like a full third of the glass?? IT'S NOT EVEN YOURS Whereee are you getting this audacity. After this moment I am in full support of the US's 21+ drinking age. Like everyone knows they're drinking but I think it needs to be confined to shitty house parties, frats, and Dirty 6th. Keep college freshmen OUT of my $5 skinny happy hour margarita! They haven't put in the work yet!

I've given up on my Guinness. It's hers now. I hope she enjoyed it. Anyway, I'm not even halfway through my Corona before flatmate decides we have to leave because his friends are at another bar. Well, we get to the bar and we finally meet his friend! And then his drunk ass gets stuck talking to some random guy and I'm making awkward eye contact with this girl I just met. Turns out this girl is the Hinge date and she traveled from rural Scotland to go out with my flatmate tonight. It's chill because she has a whole group of friends though, so I finally have some people to talk to. In the blink of an eye, though, somehow they all DISAPPEAR. They've all vanished into thin air and all of a sudden I'm third wheeling my flatmate and his Hinge date. Cold dread washes over me. I understand now that I have been cucked to the extreme. I am the biggest cuck in the entire Grampians. They're going to deport me for being such a fucking cuck.

Look, it's sad that I even ended up in this position, but at least I cut my losses at this point of the night. I begin the long, cold, walk home. My headphones are dead. Who gafs anymore? Tonight has already sucked total balls. And everyone's costume was a total fugly Spirit Halloween mess! I mean, how many polyester prisoners and banana suits can a guy take? At least it's so late into the night that the Texas-Mississippi State game is on. I stop at the corner store and buy a candy bar, then queue up Streameast and play the game on speaker out loud into the night. We win 31-3, which is a slightly positive end to an otherwise miserable night. (To be fair, if such a horrible night concluded with us losing to Mississippi fucking State, I would have killed myself. There is no coming back from losing to Mississippi State.)
Dare I say it? I miss gay Halloween.
